The basket page of the shop showed a total that was a hundred times too big: an order of 12.50 EUR showed as 1250.00 EUR. The orders service gave the total in cents under the name `total`, and the page took it for euros. The fix gives the field a clear name and unit in the service (`totalCents`, with the `currency`), and makes the page read that field. Neither change is right alone: the page needs the new field, and the service needs the page to read it.
